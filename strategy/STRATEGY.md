# STRATEGY.md — Template

> Replace every section below with content specific to your product. The cron loads this file on every draft run. If you leave it as the template, your posts will read like a template.

---

## Problem

What problem does your product solve? List 3–5 specific pain points your buyer feels. Each one should be a sentence a real customer would write in a support thread, not a generic category.

Example (replace):

- Developers waste weeks learning [X technology] syntax and configuring infrastructure.
- High costs from running [Y workloads] with unpredictable scaling.
- Limited flexibility in existing solutions that only offer fixed presets.

## Promise

What's the headline outcome you deliver? One sentence. Then 3–5 specific deliverables that back it up.

Example (replace):

**Headline:** Ship [X capability] in hours, not weeks.

**Deliverables:**

- [Specific deliverable 1]
- [Specific deliverable 2]
- [Specific deliverable 3]

## Process

How does the Promise actually get delivered? Plain mechanics, not marketing copy. 5–7 bullets.

Example (replace):

- [Capability 1 — concrete mechanism]
- [Capability 2 — concrete mechanism]
- [Pricing/usage model]

## Person

Who buys this? List 3–5 personas, **ordered by who actually buys most**. Re-rank quarterly based on signup attribution data.

Example (replace):

1. **[Primary persona]** — what they do, what they care about, what triggers them to look for a solution.
2. **[Secondary persona]** — same shape, smaller share of conversions.
3. **[Pivot-test persona]** — who you'd LIKE to sell to. Track separately; don't let the writer optimize for this group until the data justifies it.
