# READER.md — Template

> A tight persona description for the writer to calibrate against. The cron loads this on every draft run. Keep it under 200 words.

---

## Who they are

Replace with a one-paragraph description of the specific person who reads your blog. Their role, their tools, their context, the trigger that brought them to a search engine.

Example (replace):

A [role] working on [project type]. They use [tools]. They just hit [specific trigger — an error, a limit, a confusing experience]. They Googled [example query] and landed on your post.

## What they care about

3–5 bullets on what this reader will value in a post:

- [Specific value 1 — e.g., working code they can copy]
- [Specific value 2 — e.g., a quick decision: should I use X or Y?]
- [Specific value 3 — e.g., a way to avoid a common trap]

## What they do NOT care about

3–5 bullets on what to leave out:

- [Generic background context they already know]
- [Marketing language about the product itself]
- [Long history of the technology — they want the answer]

## Voice they trust

One sentence describing the voice that earns trust with this reader.

Example (replace):

A senior engineer talking to a peer. Direct, specific, no fluff. Will admit when something is hard or doesn't work yet.
